04 · Roadmap

Building the digital layer.

A phased approach allows the platform to expand from immersive visualization into a connected digital ecosystem.

Phase 01 · Foundation

Photogrammetry & Digital Capture

High-resolution 360° capture of retail units, streets, landmarks and destination assets.

Phase 02 · Current

Interactive Digital Layers

Live product information, interactive hotspots, virtual tours, commerce experiences and video.

Phase 03 · Expansion

Connected Environment

IoT sensors, environmental data and real-time foot-traffic insights can extend the digital twin.

Phase 04 · Scale

Destination Network

Replicate the platform model across additional destinations and create a connected tourism ecosystem.
